# Decisions for Angel

- Typography, overall visual direction, and the provisional full-colour KwaZulu-Natal heritage visual or a replacement photograph. Clear publication rights for any supplied photo.
- Final slogan, mission and vision wording, founder biography, and the approved account of the Makhuzwayo family heritage.
- Official HTTPS domain, public contact details, any permitted registration details, and approved social links.
- Submission eligibility, review procedure, applicant communications, any fees and participation commitments.
- Intellectual property, confidentiality, terms, privacy and POPIA handling, retention, information contact and any applicable notice requirements.
- Investor participation and regulatory review. Obtain consent and verify evidence before publishing any partner, project, achievement, ownership or investor information.

The Phase 1 pages use provisional copy, the supplied logo and a cream-and-blue interface alongside full-colour imagery. The organisation is presented independently. The website has no assumed addresses, contact numbers, partners, achievements or ownership statements.
